I co-founded ConNextor with [Elva Young](https://www.linkedin.com/in/elvayoung/) and [David Song](https://www.linkedin.com/in/wintermelons/) during ours studies at the University of Waterloo.

**The mission was to connect students from different disciplines to collaborate on projects together.** Hackathons are a great platform to meet people and hack on things in a short time frame, but we found that most projects stopped progress after the hackathon. We wanted to foster collaboration for longer term projects.

ConNextor was a social platform for students to gain and track experiences by collaborating on projects together. We built a team of 7 and had an office space in downtown Waterloo.

![ConNextor team workspace](/images/connextor.jpg)

We worked on ConNextor in parallel to our studies and internships for 2 years before sunsetting it. It was definitely one of the highlights of my university journey.
